one year ago today…

Posted on April 23, 2011 by imadeitso

…i sat down in this very same spot at the dining room table, set up a wordpress account and wrote my first post.

(so uh…did you bring me a gift? no? oh. no worries i guess. but there is something you can do for me. please read on…)

to celebrate, i’ve decided to put myself out of my comfort zone. some celebration huh?

well, what i mean is, i’d like to try new things and to put myself out there a little. i love what i do here, and i love what has happened since that time i first clicked “publish.” the ideas, inspiration and the community that has grown here is amazing. if you enjoy your time here, please consider nominating me for the canadian weblog awards.

it’s a one-time thing, i will not be asking you to vote for me, click this or that or any such nonsense. my understanding is that the nominees are judged by a panel of bloggers and blog enthusiasts. so once the nomination is made, it is out of your hands.

(and to be honest, i’m only asking because i can’t nominate myself. they have ninjas in their url and i think they’d find out!)

the only thing i need from you is a nomination, in whatever categories you feel fit this blog. both canadians and non-canadians are encouraged to send in nominations. the form can be found here.

and well, it’s been a great year and i’m looking forward to what the next year will bring. if you’ve been here before, thanks for being here again. if it’s your first time, i hope you’ll return. we’re a lovely bunch here, you’ll see.
