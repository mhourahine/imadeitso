new feature: friday archive dive

Posted on November 12, 2010 by imadeitso

one of my favourite things to do when i discover a new blog is to read back a bit into the archives. what initially attracts me to a blog is usually the most current post, through a link or topic that might be of interest to me. but when i start digging back into the archives, the style and personality of the author comes through.

the thing i enjoy about reading blogs is that it is about perspective. it’s one person’s take on something and how they chose to present it. there are some amazing and inspiring ideas out there, and some of the best posts are hidden deep within the archives. it’s just the nature of blogging, a very chronological, linear thing. you write a new post, and your last one shifts down further into the past.

it’s staggering to think that about 175 000 new blogs are started. each day!  there is absolutely no way i could read through all the archives of my favourite blogs, let alone all the potential blogs out there which i’m sure i’ll bump into in the future. but, i’m thinking, i can get to know the ones who pass through here a little better, one topic at a time…and you can too, by diving into the old archives here every friday.

blog authors hold great value in past writings, even though we are mostly focused on what we are writing now and what we might share in the future. how do i know? almost all blogs have a little “archives” tab somewhere. and we all have a slight panic when it seems that some glitch in the matrix may have caused our blog to disappear. worst nightmare, anyone? (back that thing up!)

it is, after all, a web log. a journal. a journey of sorts. we write about a topic because it means something to us at a given time. and it’s interesting to look back sometimes, and be reminded of where we’ve been.

so, where to begin? why not at the start. this week, dig up your very first post, and share it using the linky tool below. (if you’re not familiar with these, just click the blue frog button at the bottom and it should walk you through the steps. just remember to link to the actual blog post you wish to share, not just your blog url.) you can click through and read other posts on the same topic. then visit back each friday for a new topic. we’ll see how it goes.

that’s it!

that’s it ana?

that’s it.

what, no rules about content or about linking back here or leaving a comment before leaving?

must you suck the fun out of everything?

people want to know!

no rules unless they become needed. just share in the spirit of sharing, stay on topic, be nice, don’t spam, don’t beg others to follow you (ick), viagra guy you will be deleted. you get the idea.

share. read. enjoy. comment if you like. you can copy the code below if you want to help spread the word.  jump in, the water is nice…
