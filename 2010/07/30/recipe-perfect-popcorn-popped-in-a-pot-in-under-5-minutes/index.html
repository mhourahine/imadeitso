recipe: perfect popcorn popped in a pot in under 5 minutes?

Posted on July 30, 2010 by imadeitso

mmm, popcorn.

can i make a bowl of popcorn without using a microwave, in less than five minutes? let’s see…

gather my ingredients.

do some gentle stretching (spandex optional).

ready, set, go!

i pour oil into pot, just enough to cover the bottom, then add the corn. the amount depends on the size of the pot. again, just enough corn to cover the bottom of the pot.

i place the lid on the pot, and put the pot on the stove, medium-high heat.

i wait.

i start hearing the popcorn pop. i don’t open the lid, but just keep listening. once the popping slows to about 1 pop per second, i give the pot a gentle jiggle to let any unpopped kernels settle to the bottom.

once the popping slows right down, but before i smell something burning … i remove it from the heat.

time!

gah! not quite under 5 minutes. but perfectly popped corn in a pot (with only 2 ingredients, 3 if i want to add salt)… in 5 minutes. not bad at all.

you know you want some…

a few notes:

  • the kind of oil you use matters. as much as i love using extra virgin olive oil for most of my cooking, it does have a low smoke point. meaning it doesn’t take much heat for it to start burning. i use extra light olive oil or canola oil, both of which have a higher smoke point than extra virgin olive oil. you can geek out about it here.
  • the kind of kernels you use matters. this is the fun part. the bulk food store has different kinds. try them out and see which you like best.
  • the kind of pot you use doesn’t really matter. i used a cast iron pot, which takes longer to heat up. i probably could’ve come in under 5 minutes with a stainless steel pot (rookie mistake!). i don’t use non-stick, but if i did, i’d hesitate to pop corn in it because i think the kernels may scratch the coating. ok, so maybe the type of pot you use does matter.
  • every instruction i’ve read on how to make popcorn in a pot insists that you leave the lid open slightly to let the steam escape. i’ve never done this. as long as there is a little gap around the rim for the pressure to escape, i think it works better if you leave the lid on and let the steam and everything else stay inside.

mmm, popcorn (did i say that already?)…

how do you make your popcorn? favourite toppings?

it’s a long weekend here for us… enjoy yours!
